## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **2021 Coca-Cola Retail Sales Dataset** using **Google Sheets**. The objective is to identify the key factors that influence **Total Sales** through data cleaning, pivot table analysis, and interactive visualizations.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

### 📍 Regional Performance

- West region generated the highest total sales.
- Midwest recorded the lowest contribution.

**Recommendation**

Focus marketing campaigns and inventory planning on high-performing regions while investigating reasons for lower sales in Midwest.

---

### 🏪 Retailer Performance

- Sodapop contributed more than 50% of total revenue.
- DreamCo generated the lowest sales.

**Recommendation**

Reduce dependency on a single retailer by strengthening partnerships with other retailers.

---

### 🥤 Beverage Performance

- Coca-Cola is the highest-selling beverage.
- Fanta generated the lowest sales.

**Recommendation**

Maintain strong inventory for Coca-Cola while creating promotional campaigns for lower-performing beverages.

---

### 📅 Monthly Trend

- Sales peaked during July and December.
- Sales were comparatively lower during the early months of the year.

**Recommendation**

Increase inventory and marketing before seasonal demand peaks.

---

### 📦 Units Sold

- Coca-Cola recorded the highest units sold.
- Demand trends were similar across all beverage brands.

**Recommendation**

Optimize production planning based on seasonal demand.

---

# 📌 Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-performing regions.
- Improve sales strategies for low-performing regions.
- Reduce dependency on a single retailer.
- Promote underperforming beverage brands.
- Prepare inventory before seasonal sales peaks.
- Use sales trends for better demand forecasting.
